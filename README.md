## 📌 Project Overview
This repository contains an **exploratory data analysis (EDA)** of a mental health survey dataset.  
The goal is to understand how **workplace culture, family history, treatment, and resources** influence willingness to seek help.

---
📊 Key Features

- Data cleaning and preprocessing with **pandas**  
- Feature engineering for categorical survey responses  
- Visualizations with **Seaborn & Matplotlib**  
- Analysis of workplace interference, family history, and treatment factors  
- Saved figures for reporting and presentations

 🎯 Insights
1. Supportive workplace resources increase willingness to seek help.

2. High workplace interference reduces openness to treatment.

3. Family history and prior treatment strongly correlate with willingness.
